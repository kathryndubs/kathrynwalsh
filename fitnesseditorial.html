Health & Performance: Digital Manual

The challenge of this project was to design and create a fitness brand and guide. The guide must be functional as a physical and a digital manual.

As this was a fairly large project, for my process I started by dividing the material into sections and planing out my pages. From there, I could could better visualize the layouts. Where I could play with space and how many illustrations would need to be created. As an artist, I often like to give myself boundaries to work within. For this particular project, the boundries I gave myself were working within a limited colour palette and using jagged edges.

As this was the first project where I incorporated animated elements, I've had some thoughts on how I would further develop this piece. In the future I would like to push what InDesign is capable of in my next iteration. Bring more of my animation background into the development. Beyond that, I do think I was successful in creating a visually interesting design with fun animated elements to keep the reader engaged and interested in the product.
