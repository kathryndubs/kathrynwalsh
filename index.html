about me

Kathryn is a versatile graphic designer and artist based out of Toronto. Influenced by her previous work in fine arts, animation, and VFX, she is able to break convention while bringing a diverse perspective to all projects. Kathryn’s strength lies with her dedication of making inclusive and accessible designs, as well as tiptoeing the line between nature and whimsical pieces. Many of Kathryn’s designs include bold colours and a process that often has handmade elements bringing a humanist quality to her design.

Usually pulling inspiration from previous art and design movements and juxtaposing that with ideas from contemporary design trends, she believes that good design should be able to excite people across generations.

Previously worked on the following TV programmes:

  • Xavier Riddle and the Secret Museum
  • Sago Mini Friends
  • Open Season: Call of Nature
