Between Then and Now: Album Composite

For this project, I was to create a marketing package and album design for an artist. This composite is a way to showcase several of my abilities as a graphic designer and digital artist.

The musician Irish Millie is an artist who is based in my hometown. Like many of my works, I love to pull from local flora and fauna, so my inspiration stemmed from a photograph I took in my neighbourhood as a launch point for the album cover. I wanted it to feel handmade and emphasise the concept of the 'passage of time' with the imagery of trees and seasons. Rough sketches can be seen below.

In addition to creating the artwork for the album, I was tasked with creating a website and marketing package highlighting the artist. I created both a Low-Fidelity and High-Fidelity site map using Adobe Photoshop before publishing a simple website of four pages.

The marketing package includes photos and information about the artist, information about the album, as well as contact information.
